## Dictionaries

### Takeaway
There is one main online reference: dictionaryniue.com, a free digitisation of Sperlich's 1997 *Tohi Vagahau Niue* (about 10,000 entries). The Ministry for Pacific Peoples recommends it as the authority for spelling. Pollex-Online offers searchable subsets of both Sperlich (1997) and McEwen (1970). I found no evidence of a Glosbe, Wiktionary or app-based Niuean dictionary of comparable quality.

### Cited Findings
- **dictionaryniue.com** [VERIFIED-INDEX; fetch blocked] is an online digitisation of *Tohi Vagahau Niue / Niue Language Dictionary* by Wolfgang B. Sperlich, compiled with the Government of Niue between 1993 and 1997. The Ministry for Pacific Peoples recommends it as the main reference for spelling. — [MPP/MPIA orthography guidelines (PDF)](https://mpia.govt.nz/assets/Resources/Orthography-guidelines/orthography-guidelines-niue.pdf)
- Sperlich (1997), *Tohi vagahau Niue*: Niuean–English entries with an English–Niuean finderlist, about 10,000 Niuean entries, published in print by the Government of Niue and the Institute of Pacific Studies. It can be found in libraries through Open Library. — [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/works/OL18847590W/Tohi_vagahau_Niue); [Open Library edition](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL1007413M)
- **Pollex-Online** (Max Planck Institute) [VERIFIED-INDEX] lets you browse Sperlich 1997 entries with English glosses. It is a free linguistics database built for comparative work, not a learner dictionary. — [Pollex: Sperlich 1997](https://pollex.eva.mpg.de/source/219/)
- **McEwen (1970), *Niue Dictionary*** (Department of Maori and Island Affairs, Wellington, xxiv+386 pp., about 4,000 entries). It used "ng" where Niuean uses "g", which Niueans largely rejected; the Niue Assembly passed a motion deploring "ng". Entries are on Pollex. It is historical and best avoided as a primary reference for spelling. — [Pollex: McEwen 1970](https://pollex.eva.mpg.de/source/147/); [Glottolog](https://glottolog.org/resource/reference/id/319065); [Wikipedia: Niuean language](https://wikipedia.com/wiki/ISO_639:niu)
- The MPP/MPIA orthography guidelines PDF is a free, short guide to spelling conventions, including macrons. — [Orthography guidelines (PDF)](https://mpia.govt.nz/assets/Resources/Orthography-guidelines/orthography-guidelines-niue.pdf)

### Inferences
- The best dictionary setup for a learner is dictionaryniue.com for daily lookups, plus a print copy of Sperlich from a library or second-hand seller if possible. Pollex is only useful for checking a word's etymology.
- McEwen's "ng" spellings will clash with modern hymn books and Bible orthography, so learners should know about the difference.

### Gaps
- I could not open dictionaryniue.com to check its features (audio, English-to-Niuean search, mobile usability) or whether it is still online. Its domain must be added to the session's network allowlist to check.
- I found no search results confirming Glosbe or Wiktionary coverage of Niuean, or a dedicated Niuean dictionary app. Their quality is unknown.
- I did not find out whether Sperlich is currently in print, or its price.

## Government and education materials (NZ Ministry of Education, MPP, Niue Government)

### Takeaway
New Zealand government agencies publish the richest free material: curriculum guidelines, the *Haia!* beginner course (20 units with audio and video), dual-language readers with MP3s, phrase cards and Language Week kits. On the Niue side, Tāoga Niue and the Vagahau Niue Commission are the official language authorities, but I found no learner materials they publish online.

### Cited Findings
- **Haia! An Introduction to Vagahau Niue** (Ministry of Education) [VERIFIED-INDEX]: 20 units of three lessons each, with video and audio support, aimed at Years 7–8 (some listings say 7–10). The PDF is free on TKI; print copies are item 118306. Six companion storybooks are item MOE11625 (orderable by schools and education providers). It is the best fit for a structured adult beginner course. — [NZ Curriculum: Haia!](https://newzealandcurriculum.tahurangi.education.govt.nz/haia-an-introduction-to-vagahau-niue/5637211348.p); [TKI Pasifika Vagahau Niue](https://pasifika.tki.org.nz/Pacific-languages/Vagahau-Niue)
- **Tau Hatakiaga ma e Vagahau Niue: The Niue Language Guidelines** (Ministry of Education, 2012) [VERIFIED-INDEX]: a free PDF curriculum framework with levelled achievement objectives. It suits learners who want to plan their own progression. — [Niue Language Guidelines (PDF)](https://pasifika.tki.org.nz/content/download/1677/10129/file/The+Niue+Language+Guidelines.pdf); [Teaching and learning vagahau Niue](https://newzealandcurriculum.tahurangi.education.govt.nz/5637167099.p)
- **Ponataki: Niue Language Educational Resource** [VERIFIED-INDEX]: a free PDF of useful phrases. It includes "Liogi he Iki" (The Lord's Prayer) on page 45, which makes it directly useful for the prayer goal. — [Ponataki (PDF)](https://pasifika.tki.org.nz/content/download/2963/21181/file/Ponataki+-+Niue+Language+Educational+Resource.pdf)
- **TKI storybooks** [VERIFIED-INDEX]: free PDFs of Niuean picture books, such as *Ma Maia*, *Ko e Heigoa* and *Fakatai La!* with teacher notes. These are graded beginner reading. — [Ma Maia (PDF)](https://pasifika.tki.org.nz/content/download/3057/21589/file/Ma+Maia.pdf); [Ko e Heigoa (PDF)](https://pasifika.tki.org.nz/content/download/3059/21595/file/Ko+e+Heigoa.pdf); [Storybook 3](https://pasifika.tki.org.nz/Pasifika-languages/Vagahau-Niue/Storybooks-content/Storybook-3); [Fakatai La! - **Niue Language Week resources (MPP/MPIA)** [VERIFIED-INDEX]:
  - Free **Niue Language Cards** PDFs from 2023 and 2024 contain everyday phrases, plus a short prayer and hymn for opening a meeting or day.
  - The Language Week page offers social media graphics and posters for download.
  - [Language Cards 2024 (PDF)](https://mpia.govt.nz/assets/2024-pacific-language-weeks/Niue-language-week/Niue-language-cards-2024.pdf); [Language Cards 2023 (PDF)](https://www.mpp.govt.nz/assets/2023-PLW-assets/Niue-LW/Niue-Language-Cards-web-Mar2023.pdf); [MPP Niue Language Week](https://www.mpp.govt.nz/programmes/pacific-language-weeks/niue-language-week/); [MPIA Niue Language Week](https://mpia.govt.nz/programmes-and-funding/pacific-languages/pacific-language-weeks/niue-language-week)

  - The 2026 week has already passed. The next one is around October 2027, about day 330 or later of a plan starting now, so it falls outside a 180-day window starting in late November 2026.
  - [Christchurch City Libraries](https://my.christchurchcitylibraries.com/niue-language-week); [Twinkl 2026](https://www.twinkl.com/event/niue-language-week-2026)

- **NCEA Vagahau Niue** (Levels 1–4): past exams and assessment materials are a source of graded reading and listening practice. — [NZQA Vagahau Niue](https://www2.nzqa.govt.nz/ncea/subjects/select-subject/vagahau-niue/); [NZQA About](https://www.nzqa.govt.nz/ncea/subjects/vagahau-niue/)
- Civil Defence and other NZ agencies publish Niuean translations, which can serve as authentic adult reading. — [Civil Defence factsheet in Niuean (PDF)](https://www.civildefence.govt.nz/assets/Uploads/aog-welfare-factsheet/translations/niuean/where-you-can-get-support_v1_niuean.pdf)

### Inferences
- A suggested free core kit: *Haia!* as the course spine, the Language Cards for phrases, Ponataki for prayers and phrases, TKI storybooks and dual-language MP3 readers for reading and listening, and the Guidelines for setting progression goals.
- Note that "Tufuga Efakoaaga" (named in the brief) did not show up in any search. It may be a misremembered title.

## Courses (universities, community, online, Australia)

### Takeaway
Formal adult courses are scarce. I found no current Vagahau Niue paper at the University of Auckland or AUT in the search results. There is a Level 3 qualification at MIT (Manukau Institute of Technology), but its availability is unclear. For self-study, the free online course learnniue.com is the main option. I found **no Niuean classes in Australia**.

### Cited Findings
- **learnniue.com** ("Learn Niuean Online") [VERIFIED-INDEX via search summary; fetch blocked]: a free course in graded units with online audio, plus a collection of links to other resources. Secondary sources describe it as an NZ government project but disagree on which ministry runs it. Omniglot lists a lessons site at **learnniue.co.nz**, so the correct domain needs confirming. — [Omniglot: Niuean](https://omniglot.com/writing/niuean.php); search summary referencing [jarniascyril.com guide](https://www.jarniascyril.com/?p=15399)

- **Manukau Institute of Technology**: a search summary says it offers a Level 3 qualification in vagahau Niue. But StudySpy's listing for the *NZ Certificate in Pacific Language (Niue) Level 3* says "not currently available". [UNVERIFIED / possibly not running] — [StudySpy](https://studyspy.ac.nz/courses/14920/new-zealand-certificate-in-pacific-language-niue-level-3)
- **Humanitix event "Learning Vagahau Niue in Action: Practical and Cultural Engagement"** [VERIFIED-INDEX; provider, dates and price unconfirmed]: a ticketed event listing with multiple dates. A secondary source also mentions a 10-week online beginner course (2 hours of class plus at least 1 hour of review per week, about 30 hours), with no provider named. — [Humanitix](https://events.humanitix.com/learning-vagahau-niue-in-action-practical-and-cultural-engagement)
- **AUT**: holds Niuean language research, such as Kalauni (2020), *Vagahau Niue for Teaching and Learning in NZ Schools*, and Tukimata's 2024 PhD on receptive bilingualism among NZ-born Niueans, which is very relevant to heritage learners with passive exposure. ### Inferences
- A learner in Australia should plan to self-study with NZ online materials. For real conversation practice, the realistic routes are family, church congregations (Ekalesia Kerisiano Niue parishes exist in Australian cities, but I did not verify this) and online community groups, rather than formal classes.

## Apps and digital

### Takeaway
The only dedicated, reputable app is **Fakaako e Vagahau Niue**: free, made by Kiwa Digital with the Vagahau Niue Trust in 2022. Kiwa's free **Speak Pacific** app includes Niuean as one of seven languages. I found no evidence of Duolingo, Drops, Glossika or uTalk courses for Niuean, and no YouTube or podcast lesson series turned up.

### Cited Findings
- **Fakaako e Vagahau Niue** [VERIFIED-INDEX]:
  - Free. iOS (described as an iPad app); the 2022 launch said App Store and Google Play. Seller KIWA International Ltd, 54.6 MB.
  - Content: alphabet, everyday conversations, family, body parts, numbers, colours, villages, calendar, seasons, values, proverbs.
  - Features: synchronised narration, tap-to-hear pronunciation, recording your own voice.
  - Funded by the Ministry of Education's Pacific Education Innovation Fund; co-developed by the Vagahau Niue Trust and Takatakai Moana.
  - [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/sr/app/fakaako-e-vagahau-niue/id1587065389); [RNZ 2022](https://www.rnz.co.nz/international/pacific-news/470614/niuean-language-teaching-goes-digital); [Scoop launch release](https://www.scoop.co.nz/stories/BU2207/S00135.htm); [MPIA: Tool aims to sustain vagahau Niue](https://mpia.govt.nz/latest-news/tool-aims-to-sustain-vagahau-niue/); [Tagata Pasifika](https://tpplus.co.nz/education/new-app-launched-to-preserve-vagahau-niue-language)
- **Speak Pacific** (Kiwa Digital) [VERIFIED-INDEX]: a free introductory app covering seven Pacific languages, including Vagahau Niue, rated 4+. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/speak-pacific/id6448711725)
- Bible and church apps: the "Tohitapu" app in search results is **Tongan**, not Niuean, so it should not be recommended. — [App Store: Tohitapu](https://apps.apple.com/nz/app/tohitapu/id1198069267)

- Duolingo, Drops, Glossika and uTalk almost certainly have no Niuean, given that nothing came up and the language has very few speakers. But this rests on the absence of search results, not on checking each catalogue.
